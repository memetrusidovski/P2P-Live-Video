## Candidate findings

**A. (Medium, spec) A fold and a resize due at the same tick are unordered, and folding first parks the swarm at 720p until N_relay grows by 50 %.**
Inputs: the walk at t = 80.5 (T26): N_relay = 31 ≥ 30 so the ladder wants M = 6; tree 3 (L1 at 1.5 Mbps, 8 Mbps relays, U_L1(5) = 9.61) has been starving 42 % of viewers for 25 s so the fold rule's 30 s mean ŝ_3 ≈ 0.4 > 0.05; the 30 s dwell from the t = 50.5 announcement expires for both at once. Quote (Ch1 §1.1.5 §5.6): "If for some layer l < l_top the mean of ŝ_m over the last tau_fold of acknowledgements exceeds s_fold = 0.05 for any tree m carrying l, the source emits a MANIFEST_UPDATE (§1.2.4 §4.5) with the same M"; and (Ch1 §1.2.1 §1.4) "M increases at the ladder value N_up(m)"; and "The forest dwell tau_forest = 30 s applies between any two matrix changes, fold, unfold or resize alike". Nothing says which fires when both are due. Numbers: resize first → M = 6 (2,2,2), L1 at 0.75 needs 5.76 ≤ 8, 31 viewers at ≥ 720p and 9 at 1080p (T28R). Fold first → M = 5 (3,2), then M = 6 (3,3) at t = 110.5 still folded; unfold needs N_relay ≥ 1.5 × 31 = 47 (Ch1 §1.1.5 §5.6 "only when the relay population has grown by ≥ 50 % since the fold"), so 1080p never returns although the (2,2,2) mapping at M = 6 is sustainable for every layer the fold was protecting. Two implementers diverge on whether the swarm ever shows 1080p again. Not covered by ISSUE-071 (measurement windows and dwell start) or SOLUTION-058's accepted residual ("upload improves without their number changing"): here the publisher's own resize changes the per-tree requirement from 9.61 to 5.76 Mbps and the unfold rule does not read it. The implementation checks the ladder before the fold (`node/resize.rs::forest_consider`), i.e. path R. Proposed fix: state that at a dwell expiry a due resize is evaluated before a due fold, and that a fold's starvation history is discarded at a resize; and let a resize that lowers the folded layer's U_l below the one at the fold count as an unfold trigger, or carry the folded layer count into the resize decision explicitly. Blast radius: Ch1 §1.1.5 §5.6, Ch1 §1.2.1 §1.4, App B (unfold trigger). Cluster with ISSUE-071.

**C. (Low, spec) The pre-emit slot formula is stated per new tree and does not divide the headroom among several new trees.**
Inputs: T21, u_S = 20 Mbps, 3 → 5 creates two new trees (the flash-crowd 2 → 6 creates four). Quote (Ch1 §1.2.4 §4.5 step 4): "it admits floor((u_S - R_src - E_old)/(B_m Omega)) children in each new tree during the window". Numbers: headroom 14.81 - 10.38 = 4.44 Mbps; floor(4.44/1.729) = 2 per tree × 2 trees = 6.92 Mbps, i.e. source egress 10.38 + 6.92 + 5.19 = 22.5 Mbps > u_S = 20; at 2 → 6 from a full M = 2 layout the headroom is 0.98 and the formula gives 0, so the error only appears when the headroom holds ≥ 1 child and more than one tree is new. The same section's flash-crowd paragraph says "its headroom — not the window — bounds the first generation", so the charitable reading shares the headroom; the formula sentence does not. Load-bearing: an implementer following the formula oversubscribes the source by up to (new trees - 1) × headroom for five segments at every multi-tree step. Fix: one inequality, sum over new trees of pre-emit children × B_m Omega ≤ u_S - R_src - E_old, with a stated distribution (round-robin by tree id). Blast radius: §4.5 step 4, App B K_S row.

**D. (Low, spec) The source's equal-split slot formula makes K_S fall at the 5 → 6 growth step, so the source drains base-layer children while 25–30 % of its budget is idle.**
Inputs: T28R. Quote (Ch1 §1.1.5 §5.4, App B): "K_S(m) = floor((u_S - R_src)/(M B_m Omega))"; and (Ch1 §1.2.4 §4.5) "every relay recomputes K_v(m) against the new mapping at the switch; a slot count that falls is honoured by draining (DRAIN_NOTICE, reason CAPACITY)". Numbers at u_S = 20: M = 5 → (3, 3, 1, 1, 1), M = 6 → (2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1): T1 and T2 fall 3 → 2, two `DRAIN_NOTICE(CAPACITY)` to depth-1 L0 children, committed egress after 10.38 of 14.81 Mbps. At 40 Mbps: (8, 8, 4, 4, 4) → (6, 6, 6, 6, 3, 3): four L0 drains, 31.13 of 34.81 used. The relay formula with t_v = M is exact only when every tree gets the same budget share; dividing by M B_m floors away 1/M of the budget per tree. The children re-attach warm (no freeze), but the shallowest base-layer slots in the forest are given up at a growth step for no capacity reason, which contradicts the section's own intent ("the base layer is ... protected by the broadcaster directly"). Fix: allocate the source's budget greedily across trees in tree-id order (base-layer trees first) rather than by equal split, or state that K_S(m) never falls at a growth step. Blast radius: Ch1 §1.1.5 §5.4, App B. Not a design choice recorded in SOLUTION-043 (it only "defined" the count).

## Spec silences assumed through

1. Whether the source's own RELAY-class registration counts toward N_relay. Assumed yes (literal reading; impl agrees). Each ladder step is then one viewer earlier than "30 relays" suggests.
2. Which of a due resize and a due fold fires first at a dwell expiry (finding A). Walked both paths.

4. The retry cadence for a tree that is both assigned and subscribed while unparented during a window: assumed the subscriber backoff (≥ 1 s) under grace, the 5 s migration cadence after.
5. How orphans of a moving relay find a slot during the window when the targets' slot counts rise only at the switch: assumed they take whatever pre-resize free slots exist and re-attach cold at the switch otherwise.
